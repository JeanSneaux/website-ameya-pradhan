Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This website is a personal, non-commercial project. I value your privacy and believe in data minimization. This policy explains what happens to your data when you visit this site.

1. Data Controller

As this is a personal website, you can contact the website owner at: a.g.pradhan@icloud.com.

2. Information We Process (Server Logs)

This website does not intentionally collect, store, or track any personal data. It does not use analytics tools, tracking pixels, or third-party cookies.

However, because the website is hosted on the internet, our infrastructure providers automatically process technical data known as server logs. This includes:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of your visit
  • The pages you look at

3. Why This Data is Processed (Infrastructure)

We use two external services to keep this website up and running securely. They act as Data Processors:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): Hosts the static files for this website. GitHub may process your IP address for security logs and website optimization.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.): Handles web traffic routing, speeds up loading times, and protects the website from cyberattacks. Cloudflare processes your IP address to filter out malicious traffic.

Legal Basis: This processing is necessary for our legitimate interest (under GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) in delivering a functional, stable, and secure website to you.

4. Cookies

This website does not use any tracking, advertising, or marketing cookies.

5. Future Contact Form (Placeholder)

The "Contact Me" page currently visible on this website is a placeholder and does not actively collect or transmit messages. No data entered there is currently processed.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data held about you. Because we do not store your data or link IP addresses to your identity, we cannot identify individual visitors.
